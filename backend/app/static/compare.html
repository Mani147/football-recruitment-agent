How reliable is this comparison?

The current dataset is hand-authored for a portfolio demonstration. It lacks independently verified source links, documented percentile cohorts and specialist goalkeeper data. Fees and wages are estimates. Multi-position listings may place the same player in several groups. League adjustments are heuristics; cross-league comparisons are approximate. Charts describe the stored records, not measured transfer success or a validated player ranking.
